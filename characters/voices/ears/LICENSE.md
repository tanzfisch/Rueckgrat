Modifications: 6–10 s excerpts, resampled 48 kHz → 24 kHz mono 16-bit, loudness-normalised (~-20 LUFS, peak ≤ -1 dBFS), 0.2 s silence padding.
Note: the licence covers the recordings; cloning a real person's voice may raise additional consent/likeness issues.
